About Pomodoro Technique

The Pomodoro Technique is a time management method developed by Francesco Cirillo in the late 1980s. The main goal of this technique is to improve focus, productivity, and work efficiency by breaking tasks into short intervals called “Pomodoros,” typically lasting 25 minutes, followed by a short break (5 minutes), and a long break (15 minutes) after four cycles.

Objectives:

  • To help users maintain focus and reduce procrastination.
  • To increase productivity through structured work and rest cycles.
  • To encourage effective time tracking and self-discipline.

Benefits:

  • Improves concentration and prevents burnout.
  • Enhances task management and prioritization.
  • Builds consistent work habits and motivation.
